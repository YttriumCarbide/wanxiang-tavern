# 艾尔鲁斯场景与中转站

艾尔鲁斯是艾尔德兰帝国的首都。城内规划十段同尺度、可横向拖动的长景，约为星钟城五段主要街景的两倍。城内路线交叉成环，返回城门可走另一条街。跨城入口使用大地图的艾尔鲁斯线。

## 画风与构图

以游戏内星灯街的 `../street-hd-v4/connected.png` 和同一套风格的单幅参考图为准。原街景提示词中的大块浅墙面、大石板、简洁屋檐、克制铺陈和宽而柔和的阴影继续使用。银星、盾牌和交叉双剑集中放在需要辨认的建筑上。

> Paint one native 3:1 anime/JRPG game panorama, following the approved Starbell street's clean hand-painted style. Keep one street-level eye height, one consistent horizon and one principal vanishing point. All near, middle and far buildings belong to the same continuous street and ground plane. Use one dominant local landmark. Finish the distance with a quiet roof line, sparse trees or soft open sky. Through arches, show low street buildings or a short lane. Remove distant castles, palaces, cliff-top buildings, mountain skylines and duplicated silhouettes. Keep large calm plaster and paving shapes, broad soft shadows, a few deliberate slate and muted burgundy accents. No dense masonry seams, granular pavement, leaf speckles, noisy foliage or high-frequency decoration. Keep doorways and walking lanes open at the specified positions. Leave the lower pavement clear for character sprites. No lettering, people, watermark or UI.
